This directory contains proposals for `prov` — documents that argue for a
change, and may lose the argument.

A proposal is not a to-do list and not a source of truth. What shipped is
documented in [the spec](/docs/spec.md), [DESIGN](/docs/DESIGN.md) and the
guides, never here; a commitment to consumers is a changelog entry, never here.

A resolved proposal keeps its place in the list above, because a document
nothing reaches is a `check` finding and because the reasoning is the value even
when it lost. Its outcome is in its `status` — `draft`, `accepted`,
`implemented`, `deferred`, or `rejected` — and in a **Status** section at the top
naming the release that settled it. The body is left as it was argued.
